Make It Right PayPal sandbox

Paid a contractor up front, and the job was left half done?

Make It Right reads the estimate, the text messages and the job-site photos, sorts every promised item into done, partly done or not done with a photo for each, and prepares a PayPal claim for exactly the unfinished work. You file the claim yourself in PayPal; the app follows the contractor's offer, the escalation and the refund. It never moves money on its own.

Photo check: recorded analysis by Gemma 4 31B (run locally on 2026-11-06); the hosted demo replays it. PayPal calls are live, sandbox only.

Judging Make It Right? Try it in about 3 minutes

  1. Press the button: the app makes your own copy of the demo case and opens PayPal's sandbox.
  2. Log in with your PayPal sandbox Personal account and approve the payment. Sandbox money only.
  3. Back here, press "File with PayPal" and file "Service not received" for the amount shown.
  4. The app finds your claim within a few minutes (3 to 4 in our sandbox runs). Have the contractor stand-in make an offer, then accept it on PayPal or ask PayPal to decide.

You need a PayPal sandbox Personal account; you can create one in the PayPal Developer Dashboard, under Sandbox accounts. The contractor is played by our sandbox business account.
